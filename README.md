## 🎯 Business Problem

A leading retail company wants to better understand its customers' shopping behaviour to improve **sales, customer satisfaction and long-term loyalty**. Management has noticed changing purchase patterns across demographics, product categories and sales channels, and wants to know which factors (discounts, reviews, seasons, payment preferences) drive customer decisions and repeat purchases.

**Core question:**
> *How can the company leverage consumer shopping data to identify trends, improve customer engagement and optimise marketing and product strategies?*

## 🏁 Objectives

- Prepare a reliable, analysis-ready dataset from raw shopping data.
- Answer **nine** specific business questions about customer behaviour.
- Deliver an **interactive Power BI dashboard** for ongoing monitoring.
- Provide clear, data-backed **recommendations** for marketing, promotions and loyalty.

## 📂 Dataset

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| File | `customer_shopping_behavior.csv` |
| Records | 3,900 customer transactions |
| Attributes | 18 (customer profile, product, purchase, engagement) |
| Total revenue | $233,081 |
| Average purchase | $59.76 (range $20 – $100) |
| Average review rating | 3.75 / 5 |
| Gender split | Male 68% · Female 32% |
| Subscribers | 27% of customers |
| Transactions with a discount | 43% |

**Attribute groups:** customer profile (age, gender, location) · product (item, category, size, colour) · purchase (amount, season, payment method, shipping type) · engagement (review rating, subscription, discount, previous purchases, purchase frequency).

## 🔍 Key Findings

| Area | Result | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Gender** | Male $157,890 (67.7%) vs Female $75,191 (32.3%) | The gap comes from customer *volume*. Average spend is almost equal ($59.54 vs $60.25). |
| **Discounts** | 839 discount users spent above the $59.76 average (avg $79.79) | Discounts do not shrink basket value. |
| **Ratings** | Gloves 3.86 · Sandals 3.84 · Boots 3.82 · Hat 3.80 · T-shirt 3.78 | Satisfaction is consistent across products. |
| **Shipping** | Express $60.48 vs Standard $58.46 | Express buyers spend about 3.5% more. |
| **Subscription** | Subscribers $59.49 vs non-subscribers $59.87 | Subscription does not lift spend per order. |
| **Discount share** | Hat 50.0% · Sneakers 49.7% · Coat 49.1% · Sweater 48.2% · Pants 47.4% | Promotions are concentrated on a few products. |
| **Segments** | New 83 · Returning 1,882 · Loyal 1,935 | Mature customer base with weak new-customer inflow. |
| **Top products** | Jewelry, Pants and Blouse lead (171 orders each) | No single dominant product within a category. |
| **Repeat buyers** | 27.6% subscribe vs 22.4% for other customers | About 2,500 repeat buyers are an untapped subscription audience. |

## 💡 Recommendations

- **Acquire new customers.** Only 2.1% of customers are new, so invest in acquisition and referral campaigns.
- **Grow the female customer base.** They already spend slightly more per order.
- **Keep discounts, but target them.** Reward the 839 high-spending discount users and review margins on heavily discounted products.
- **Launch a subscription campaign** for unsubscribed repeat buyers and **redesign subscriber benefits** so they lift spend.
- **Promote best-rated products** (Gloves, Sandals, Boots) and **encourage Express shipping**.
- **Recalibrate the VIP tier.** No customer currently exceeds 50 previous purchases.

## ⚠️ Limitations & Next Steps

**Limitations**
- Many differences between groups are small and were not statistically tested.
- No transaction date, so trends, retention and lifetime value cannot be measured.
- No channel or margin data, so online vs offline performance and discount profitability cannot be assessed.

**Next steps**
- Add date, channel and margin data.
- Add statistical significance testing.
- Extend the dashboard with trend and segment views.
- Explore predictive models (subscription propensity, churn).
